# New Expense — Staff Training Module

**Page:** `/admin/expenses/new` — recording a cost.
**Audience:** staff entering expenses (primary).
**Time to complete:** about 10 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the Expenses module.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can enter an expense so it sails through approval.

## 1. The flow

1. **Date spent** — the day the money went out (defaults to the business
   date).
2. **Category** — pick the truest one (Utilities, Fuel, Repairs,
   Transport, Supplies, Marketing, Staff welfare, Fees/taxes, Bank charges,
   Other). "Other" is a last resort; a miscategorized expense corrupts the
   by-category view.
3. **What it was for** — the description. Write it so a stranger understands
   in six months: *"Generator diesel, 40 litres"* beats *"fuel"*.
4. **Amount (৳)** and **Paid by** — Cash, Bank transfer, bKash, Nagad,
   Card, or Cheque.
5. **Paid to** and **supplier** (both optional) — link the listed supplier
   when there is one; it keeps the supplier's history complete.
6. **Bill, voucher or transaction number** (optional) — the paper trail.
   For anything sizable, fill it; the approver will ask otherwise.
7. **Submit for approval** — the expense enters the queue as Waiting.

## 2. Entering well

- **One expense per bill.** Don't bundle the electricity bill with the
  plumber — they belong in different categories and the approver can't
  split them.
- **Dates are real dates.** Backdate to when it was spent, not when you're
  entering it — the month view depends on it.
- **Descriptions are for the future.** The approver, the auditor, and you
  in six months all read the same line.

## 3. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| Submitted but nothing happened | It's in "Waiting for approval" — a manager approves it. That's the workflow. |
| Wrong category after submitting | Edit it (detail page) before approval — after approval, ask a manager. |
| Paid from the cash drawer? | Expenses never touch cash shifts — record the payment method (Cash) on the expense instead. |
