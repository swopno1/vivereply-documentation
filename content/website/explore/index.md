# Explore — Staff Training Module

**Pages:** `/destinations`, `/experiences`, `/explore-the-area`, `/dining`,
`/amenities`, `/gallery`, `/offers`.
**Audience:** all staff.
**Time to complete:** about 15 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the [Website](..) module.

## Learning objectives

After this module you know what each explore section shows and the
operational truth behind it.

## 1. The sections

| Page | What guests see |
|---|---|
| `/destinations` (+ `/destinations/[slug]`) | The area around the property — places to visit, each with its own page |
| `/experiences` (+ `/experiences/[slug]`) | Curated experiences: treks, boat trips, cultural visits |
| `/explore-the-area` (+ `/explore-the-area/[slug]`) | Guides to the surrounding area |
| `/dining` | The restaurant: menu highlights, hours |
| `/amenities` | Property amenities — pool, wifi, parking, and the rest |
| `/gallery` | Photos of the property |
| `/offers` | Current deals and packages |

## 2. The operational truth behind each

- **Destinations and experiences** are the front desk's upsell script.
  Know them cold — when a guest asks "what's there to do?", your answer
  should match the site. If an experience stops running, the site must
  say so the same day.
- **Dining** must match the [room-service menu](../../room-service/menu/)
  reality: what's listed as available should be orderable. Hours on the
  site = hours the kitchen works.
- **Amenities** are promises. Every item listed is something a guest can
  fairly demand. Audit this page quarterly — properties drift, pages
  don't.
- **Offers** are pricing promises with dates. An expired offer left up is
  a discount the property didn't intend. Take offers down the day they
  end.

## 3. Staff discipline

**Never contradict the site.** If the site says the restaurant opens at
7, the restaurant opens at 7. If reality must change, change the site
first (or at least the same day) — the guest who read it last night
outranks the memo from this morning.

## 4. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| Guest expects something that doesn't exist | The site promises it — either deliver it or fix the site today. |
| Offer honored after expiry | The offer page wasn't taken down — remove it and honor this one gracefully. |
| Photos don't match reality | The gallery is stale — new photos are a manager task, not a someday task. |
