# Rates & Promos — Staff Training Module

**Page:** `/admin/rooms/rates` — seasonal pricing, promo codes, deposits.
**Audience:** managers, owners.
**Time to complete:** about 15 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the [Rooms](..) module.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can explain the deposit rule, set a seasonal rate,
and manage promo codes — including who applies them.

## 1. The deposit rule

**The deposit is always 100% of the booking total.** The page states it
plainly: changing it takes a vendor task, not a setting. Every booking
collects in full — there is no part-payment plan in this version.

## 2. Seasonal rates

Multipliers over the base price per night for a date range: pick the
room (active types only), name the season, set the base price, the
adjust percent, and the dates, then **Add rate**. A seasonal rate makes
peak dates price themselves — set them before the season, not during.

## 3. Promo codes

Codes like FAMILY20: a percent off, a minimum-nights rule, and a
**Switch off / Switch on** toggle each. Creating and toggling are
audited. Two rules that prevent the classic mistakes:

- **Promos are manager-applied.** *"Promo codes are provided to a booking
  manager. They do not show in live calculations."* Guests never see or
  enter codes themselves — the manager applies the right one to the
  booking, and the discount flows from there.
- **Off means off immediately.** A switched-off code stops applying to
  new bookings at once; existing bookings keep what they had.

## 4. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| Room missing from the seasonal form | Only active room types show — check it's active. |
| Promo not discounting | Is it switched on? Does the stay meet the minimum nights? And remember: the manager applies it — it never applies itself. |
| Deposit question from a guest | Always 100% — that is the rule, not a setting you can change. |
