# Blog Categories — Staff Training Module

**Page:** `/admin/blog/categories` — organizing topics.
**Audience:** editors, managers.
**Time to complete:** about 10 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the [Blog](..) module.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can manage categories without breaking the blog's
organization.

## 1. What categories are

The blog's topic shelves: each post sits on exactly one. Guests browse by
category, and the website's navigation is built from them. Each category
has a **name**, **Bengali name**, **slug** (URL piece), **description**,
**display order**, and an **active** flag — plus a live **post count**
showing how many posts sit on the shelf.

**Permission:** managing taxonomy needs the edit permission.

## 2. Managing well

- **Few and stable.** Five to ten categories cover a property blog
  (Destinations, Food, Guides, Stories, News…). A new category for every
  third post is clutter — that's what tags are for.
- **Display order** is the shelf arrangement on the site — put the most
  useful first.
- **Deactivate, don't delete**, when a category falls out of use: its
  posts keep their history, and old links don't break. (Tags work the
  same — see [Tags](../tags/).)
- **Bengali names** matter: fill them; many guests browse in Bengali.

## 3. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| Category missing from the editor | It's inactive — reactivate it, or pick the replacement. |
| "Uncategorized" posts piling up | Writers are skipping the field — make category selection part of the publishing checklist. |
