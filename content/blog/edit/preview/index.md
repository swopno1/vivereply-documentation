# Post Preview — Staff Training Module

**Page:** `/admin/blog/[id]/preview` — the guest's-eye view.
**Audience:** writers, editors.
**Time to complete:** about 5 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the [Blog](..) module.

## Learning objectives

After this module you preview every post before it goes live — and know
what to look for.

## 1. What it is

The post rendered **exactly as guests will see it** on the website: title,
hero, body formatting, photos with their descriptions, author line,
category and tags. A preview header keeps you oriented (you're in admin,
not on the live site).

## 2. The pre-publish checklist

Open preview and check, every time:

1. **Title and excerpt** — do they read well on their own?
2. **Photos** — do they load, in the right order, each with its
   description?
3. **Formatting** — headings nested sensibly, no broken Markdown, links
   going where they claim.
4. **Author and category** — correct, and the post isn't Uncategorized.
5. **Mobile** — shrink the window; most guests read on phones.

Only then publish or approve. The preview is the cheapest quality gate
the blog has — use it.
