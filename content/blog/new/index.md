# New Post — Staff Training Module

**Page:** `/admin/blog/new` — writing in the editor.
**Audience:** content writers (primary), editors.
**Time to complete:** about 15 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the [Blog](..) module.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can write and submit a post that meets the blog's
standards.

## 1. The editor

One form for everything a post needs:

- **Title** — the headline guests see. Specific beats clever: *"3 Days in
  Cox's Bazar on a Budget"* beats *"Paradise Found"*.
- **Slug** — the URL piece, auto-derived from the title. Keep it short,
  lowercase, hyphenated.
- **Excerpt** — one or two sentences shown on listing cards and link
  previews. Write it deliberately; it's the post's advertisement.
- **Content** — the body in Markdown (headings, bold, lists, links all
  work). Write \{min}–\{max} characters per the guest guidelines' spirit:
  substantial enough to be useful, tight enough to be read.
- **Category** — one, from the managed list. Uncategorized posts look
  lost — always pick one.
- **Tags** — a few, from the managed list. Tags are how readers discover
  related posts.
- **Photos** — up to the staff image limit, each under the size limit
  (JPEG/PNG/WebP), **each with a short description**. Photos without
  descriptions are incomplete — the description is also the accessibility
  text.

## 2. Publishing paths

- **Save as draft** — keep working; invisible to guests.
- **Submit for review** — enters the [moderation queue](../pending/) as
  PENDING.
- **Publish** — goes live immediately (or on the scheduled date you set).
  Whether staff posts publish directly or need approval is a **blog
  setting** (`staffPostsAutoPublish`) — know which mode your property
  uses before you promise a publish date.

## 3. Writing well for this blog

The guest guidelines are the house standard even for staff posts: your own
trip and honest impressions, no advertising or affiliate links, no copied
text. The blog earns trust by being genuinely useful — a guide that saves
a traveler an hour is worth ten generic "visit beautiful X" posts.

## 4. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| Photo won't upload | Check the type (JPEG/PNG/WebP) and size against the blog settings limits. |
| Publish button missing | Your property may require approval — submit for review instead. |
| Slug looks wrong | Edit it before publishing; changing it after breaks shared links. |
