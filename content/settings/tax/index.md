# Tax Settings — Staff Training Module

**Page:** `/admin/settings?tab=tax` — tax configuration.
**Audience:** owners, accountants.
**Time to complete:** about 10 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the [Settings](..) module.

## Learning objectives

After this module you know what the tax tab configures and its limits.

## 1. What it is

How the property handles tax on its charges: rates, what's taxable, and
how tax appears on folios and reports. For this property, pricing is
**tax-inclusive by design** — the rate guests see is the rate they pay,
with no tax added at checkout.

## 2. Limits

This tab configures **how tax is presented and reported**, not tax advice.
Rates, filings, and compliance are the accountant's job — the system
records what it's told. If the property's tax situation changes, the
accountant says what, and this tab records it.

## 3. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| Folio totals look off | Check whether a tax setting changed recently — and whether the folio lines predate the change (history is never rewritten). |
| Tab missing | Your role can't open it — owner/accountant territory. |
