# Record Waste — Staff Training Module

**Page:** `/admin/inventory/move?kind=WASTE` — spoiled, broken, expired.
**Audience:** storekeeper (primary), managers.
**Time to complete:** about 10 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the Inventory module, [Movements overview](../).

## Learning objectives

After this module you can record waste so it becomes management
information instead of a hidden loss.

## 1. The flow

1. Add a line per item: choose the item, enter the **quantity**, and write
   the **reason** — required, every time.
2. **Add another item** as needed, review, **Save** — *"Saved. Stock
   updated."*

## 2. The reason is the whole point

Waste is a management signal: rising waste on an item means over-ordering,
bad storage, or a supplier problem. A blank, vague, or jokey reason
destroys that signal. Write what actually happened — *"milk soured, fridge
at 12°C overnight"*, *"3 plates chipped in delivery"* — so the pattern is
visible when the manager reviews waste by item.

**Never hide waste inside issues.** Issuing spoiled stock "to the kitchen"
to avoid the waste record falsifies both departmental costs and the waste
signal. Record it as waste, with the reason.

## 3. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| Save blocked with no clear error | The reason is required on every waste line — check each one. |
| Waste keeps rising on one item | Not a recording problem — escalate: ordering, storage, or supplier. |
