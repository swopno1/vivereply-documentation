# Plan Your Visit — Staff Training Module

**Pages:** `/about`, `/contact`, `/how-to-reach`, `/location`, `/faq`,
`/reviews`, `/press-kit`.
**Audience:** front desk, managers.
**Time to complete:** about 15 minutes.
**Prerequisite:** the [Website](..) module.

## Learning objectives

After this module you know what each planning page tells guests and how
it connects to your daily work.

## 1. The pages

| Page | What it does | Your part |
|---|---|---|
| `/about` | The property's story | Know it — guests will ask, and your version should match |
| `/contact` | Info grid + enquiry form | **Respond fast.** The form POSTs to `/api/contact`: the lead is saved and the team gets a Resend alert. Phone is required on the form — call, don't just email back. |
| `/how-to-reach` | Directions: road, air, local transport | Keep it current — road conditions and transport options change |
| `/location` | Map and address | The address must match every listing and the [settings](../../settings/general/) |
| `/faq` | Frequently asked questions | If guests keep asking something not here, add it — the FAQ is a living page |
| `/reviews` | Guest reviews | Reviews are earned at checkout — ask happy guests, gracefully |
| `/press-kit` | Media assets for journalists | Keep the logo and facts current; route press enquiries to the manager |

## 2. The contact form — your fastest lead

A contact enquiry is a guest raising their hand. The alert goes to the
**settings contact email** — if that's still a placeholder, leads go
nowhere. **Reply within the hour during the day.** A lead called back in
an hour books; a lead emailed tomorrow books elsewhere.

## 3. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| No contact enquiries arriving | The contact email in settings — is it real? Check the spam folder too. |
| Guest lost on the way | The how-to-reach page is stale — update it and call the guest. |
| FAQ answers contradict staff | The FAQ wins until it's fixed — then fix it the same day. |
